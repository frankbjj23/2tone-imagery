## Design takeaways for 2Tone Imagery

- Build an original black, off-white, silver, and red visual direction around the supplied logo.
- Give all six confirmed services a clear place: DTF, silk screen printing, embroidery, UV stickers, vinyl, and banners.
- Use one primary project-start action and a secondary service-exploration action.
- Explain services in plain language and help visitors choose based on the item, artwork, and intended use.
- Gather useful project details: service, quantity, dimensions, desired date, artwork readiness, and notes.
- Label illustrative mockups honestly. Do not present them as completed customer work.
- Do not reuse competitor artwork, layouts, testimonials, ratings, prices, guarantees, or production claims as 2Tone's own.

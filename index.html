GOOD QUESTIONS. CLEAR STARTING POINTS.

BEFORE
WE PRINT.

Which printing service should I choose?+

Start with the item you want to make. DTF and screen printing are options for apparel graphics; embroidery creates a stitched finish. UV stickers, vinyl and banners suit different branding applications. Describe your item, artwork and quantity so the right process can be discussed.

What should I include in my project brief?+

Include the service or item, quantity, approximate print dimensions, colors, intended surface and desired date. Mention whether your artwork is ready or still an idea. The builder helps you put these details together.

Can I use my own logo or artwork?+

Yes—start your project around your own logo or design. Tell us what file formats you have. Artwork quality, print size and any preparation needed should be reviewed before production. Use artwork you own or have permission to reproduce.

What are the prices and turnaround times?+

Pricing and timing depend on the service, materials, quantity, artwork and project requirements. Share your details and desired date with 2Tone Imagery for confirmation before placing an order.

Does the project builder place an order?+

No. It creates a brief you can copy, download or share. Nothing is automatically submitted, charged or ordered. Call 201-210-4027 with your brief to confirm the project.
